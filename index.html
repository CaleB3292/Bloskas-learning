About us

A small school with big curiosity.

Founded in 2024, the Bloskas Learning Institute offers a tailored homeschool experience with a 3:1 student–teacher ratio. Every lesson is built around the student in front of us, with plenty of room for questions, adventures, and the occasional snack break.
