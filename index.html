About Us

The Bloskas Learning Institute, established in 2024 to address a specific learning gap for a intelligent group of students, provides a highly personalized education tailored to each student’s unique needs. With a 3:1 student-to-teacher ratio, we occasionally offer ad-hoc lessons to non-enrolled children, but we are currently not accepting new students.
